Notice of Privacy Practices

Effective: July 28, 2026 · Version 1.0

This notice describes how health information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.

Who We Are and Our Role

Axeum Technologies, Inc. ("Axeum") provides governed provider intelligence services through axeumFLOW, a healthcare workflow platform operated at care.axeumai.com. Axeum acts as a Business Associate to healthcare practices (your providers) under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA), as amended by the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health (HITECH) Act.

Your healthcare provider (e.g., Mount Vernon Rehabilitation Medicine) is the Covered Entity responsible for your care and for issuing their own Notice of Privacy Practices explaining how they use your health information. This notice explains how Axeum handles health information it receives from your provider in order to support your provider's operations.

What Health Information We Process

Axeum receives and processes Protected Health Information (PHI) that your provider shares with us to deliver services, including: appointment and scheduling data; clinical documentation and encounter notes; diagnosis and procedure codes; insurance and billing information; remittance and payment data; and remote patient monitoring data where applicable.

Axeum applies the minimum-necessary standard — we access and use only the PHI required to perform the specific service your provider has engaged us to deliver.

How We Use and Disclose Health Information

Axeum uses PHI only as permitted under our Business Associate Agreement with your provider and applicable law:

  • Treatment, Payment, and Healthcare Operations. We process PHI to support your provider's clinical workflows, revenue cycle management, compliance auditing, and quality reporting.
  • AI-Assisted Analysis. When PHI is submitted to AI processing (including large language model analysis), Axeum de-identifies the information before transmission to third-party AI providers, consistent with 45 C.F.R. §164.514. Identifiable PHI is never transmitted to AI providers in identifiable form.
  • Subcontractors. Axeum may disclose PHI to sub-processors operating under Business Associate Agreements with Axeum, including: Amazon Web Services (Transcribe Medical, infrastructure), Microsoft (authentication services), Vercel (platform hosting), and Neon (database infrastructure). All sub-processors are bound by HIPAA-compliant data processing agreements.
  • Required by Law. We will disclose PHI as required by law, including in response to valid court orders, subpoenas, or regulatory requests.
  • Breach Notification. We will notify your provider of any breach of unsecured PHI as required by the HITECH Act Breach Notification Rule (45 C.F.R. Part 164, Subpart D), and your provider will notify you as required.

What We Will Not Do

Axeum will not: sell your health information; use it for marketing purposes; use it to train AI models without explicit authorization; or disclose it for purposes not permitted under our Business Associate Agreement with your provider.

Your Rights

Because Axeum is a Business Associate (not your direct healthcare provider), most patient rights under HIPAA must be exercised through your healthcare provider. Your provider is responsible for: providing access to your medical records; making amendments to your records; providing an accounting of disclosures; and honoring restriction requests. If you have questions about your rights, contact your healthcare provider directly.

Security

Axeum implements administrative, physical, and technical safeguards consistent with the HIPAA Security Rule (45 C.F.R. §§164.308–164.318), including: encrypted data transmission and storage; tenant-scoped data isolation; append-only cryptographically signed audit records; role-based access controls; and automatic session timeout.
